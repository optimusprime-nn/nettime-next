NetTime Next 0.2.0 — Windows service preview

This installer adds a separate, automatically starting Windows service named
NetTimeNext. It measures every configured NTP source and keeps seven days of
evidence while the desktop window is closed and users are logged out.

It does not change the PC clock, stop the original NetTime service, or open an
NTP server port. Your existing NetTime remains the clock controller.

NTS authentication and full NTP synchronization conformance remain pending.
Plain NTP measurements are labelled unauthenticated. NTS-required entries
are blocked rather than sent as plain requests.

This preview is not digitally signed. Installation requires administrator
approval. Configuration and history live in ProgramData\NetTimeNext and are
preserved by upgrades and uninstall.
